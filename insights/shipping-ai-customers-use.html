Shipping AI that customers actually use

Rob Chang · November 1, 2026

Most AI demos die somewhere between a happy-path screenshot and a Tuesday morning workflow. I treat that gap as the actual product.

Start from a workflow, not a model

Pick one job a person already does: caption a news package, answer a guest’s question, prep a podcast episode. Write down what “done” means in their words. Only then pick models and tools.

Measure quality in week one

Choose two things you can measure right away, like proper-noun accuracy or how often the assistant hands off to a person. If you can’t measure it, you can’t decide whether to scale it.

Plan the handoff

Docs, runbooks, and who owns the prompts after I leave are part of the work, not an afterthought.
