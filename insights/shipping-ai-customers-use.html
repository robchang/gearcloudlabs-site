Insight · 2026-11-01

Shipping AI that customers actually use

Most AI demos die in the gap between a happy path screenshot and a Tuesday morning workflow. At Gearcloud Labs we treat that gap as the product.

Start from a workflow, not a model

Pick one job a human already does: caption a package, answer a guest FAQ, prep a podcast episode. Write the acceptance criteria in their language. Only then choose models, tools, and evals.

Instrument quality early

Glossary hits, proper-noun accuracy, escalation rate, time-to-first-draft — pick two metrics you can measure in week one of a pilot. If you cannot measure it, you cannot decide to scale it.

Plan the handoff

Docs, runbooks, and who owns the prompts when we leave are part of the deliverable — not an afterthought.
