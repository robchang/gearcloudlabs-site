I started Gearcloud Labs to build AI that holds up once real customers start using it.

I’m the founder and CTO. Before this I was Head of Entertainment Applications at Amazon Smart Vehicles (Alexa Automotive), Director and Head of Applications at Arlo, and Director of Mobile Apps at Verizon. I also ran iliotech, my consulting practice, and I studied electrical engineering at Stanford.

Most of my career has been shipping apps on constrained platforms: cars, cameras, phones. That’s a useful habit for AI work, where the hard part is rarely the model and usually everything around it.
