Newsroom caption pipeline

Project notes · client details kept private

Generic speech-to-text gets most words right and the names wrong. In news, the names are the part that matters.

The approach

Video lands in a shared drive and goes to a GPU box. Speech recognition runs alongside OCR on the lower thirds, so the on-screen spelling of a person or place can correct the transcript. An LLM pass then checks proper nouns against a glossary before export.

What comes out

SRT and SCC caption files, ready for broadcast and the web.

The pieces

  • Drive or object-storage ingest
  • GPU speech recognition and OCR
  • LLM post-edit with a glossary
  • SRT and SCC export
